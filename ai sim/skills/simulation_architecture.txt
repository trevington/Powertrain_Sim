Path Commander (driver.py)
- "drives" car
- Uses feedback loop to move car to next waypoint
- Allows us to simulate car on multiple courses (Endurance, Acceleration, Autocross, Skidpad)
- Outputs: throttle % (to powertrain controller sim), steering angle (to powertrain controller sim)

Powertrain controller Sim (powertrain.py)
- Controls Powertrain
- Ensures FSAE rules are followed during simulation
- Allocates power to motor(s)
- Should simulate realistic vehicle Commander
- Outputs: Power demand (to tractive battery electrical sim), motor power distribution (to motor & inverter sim)

Tractive Battery Electrical Sim (motor.py)
- Simulates tractive battery electrical performance (efficiency, heat generation, maximum power, Open Circuit voltage, energy used) at operating point
- Treats tractive battery as point with uniform Temperature
- Outputs: Power supplied (to motor & inverter sim), heat generated (to tractive battery cooling sim)

Tractive Battery Cooling Sim (tb_thermal.py)
- Simulates tractive battery cooling performance
- Outputs: heat removed (to tractive battery electrical sim)

Motor & Inverter Sim (motor.py)
- Simulates shaft output at operating point
- Calculates energy lost, efficiency and heat generation
- Optimize car for motor performance
- Outputs: heat generation (to Motor & inverter thermal sim), shaft output (to Vehicle dynamics sim)

Motor & Inverter thermal sim (motor.py)
- Simulates radiator motor & inverter cooling
- Calculates radiator sizing
- Motor & Inverter don't need to be at steady state Temperature, but the temperature shouldn't exceed 60C during endurance
- Motor & Inverter temperatures cannot exceed 50C
- Outputs: cooling capability (to motor & inverter sim)

Vehicle dynamics Sim (vd.py)
- Should be one file so that it can be swapped when VD team creates model
- Simulates ambient conditions around car
- Runs tire sims and calculates non-powertrain losses
- Outputs: vehicle state (to Path Commander)
